# deep-dive

When to use: Dan wants a brainstorm or a deep dive on a new product or feature idea. This is the second priority.

1. State the question in one sentence.
2. Separate findings (from sources) from ideas (brainstorms). Label each idea as an idea.
3. Name open uncertainties. Do not paper over a missing source.
4. End with candidate product or feature ideas Gates can later turn into a spec. Do not file tickets, write a Claude spec, or assign Linus.
5. Deliver Markdown only, with no em dashes and no preamble.
